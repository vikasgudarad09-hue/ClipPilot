Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 09, 2026

At ClipPilot ("we", "us", or "our"), accessible via our desktop application and website, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our desktop application interacts with your Google account, YouTube channel, and personal information.

Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance:
ClipPilot's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

1. Information We Access

When you connect your YouTube channel to ClipPilot, our desktop application requests access through Google's official OAuth 2.0 authorization mechanism. We access only the permissions required to provide video scheduling services:

  • YouTube Account & Channel Information: Channel title, custom URL, and basic profile info to display your active channel connection.
  • YouTube Upload & Management Permissions (youtube.upload): Used solely to upload video content, apply custom thumbnails, set scheduled publication times, and write titles, descriptions, and tags selected by you.

2. How We Store Your Data (Local Storage Only)

Your data stays on your personal computer:

  • ClipPilot is a desktop application running directly on your Windows PC.
  • Your Google OAuth credentials, refresh tokens, and video files are stored strictly on your local machine.
  • We do not operate central cloud servers that collect, store, or view your private video files or authentication tokens.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information accessed via Google APIs is used exclusively to:

  • Upload videos you select directly to your YouTube channel.
  • Set video publication schedules according to your cadence preferences.
  • Apply custom video thumbnails and SEO metadata (titles, descriptions, tags).

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, lease, trade, or transfer your personal data or Google account information to any third parties.

  • Your user data is never used for advertising, marketing campaigns, or targeting.
  • Your data is never used to train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

5. How to Revoke Access

You remain in complete control of your account access at all times. You can disconnect your YouTube channel from ClipPilot by:

  1. Clicking "Change / Disconnect" within the ClipPilot desktop application.
  2. Or revoking ClipPilot's access directly from your Google Security Settings at: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

6. Security

ClipPilot uses standard HTTPS encryption when communicating with Google's official APIs. Tokens stored locally on your machine are secured by your operating system's standard file permissions.
