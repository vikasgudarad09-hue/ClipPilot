# ✈️ ClipPilot (Made in India 🇮🇳)
### Put Your Video Publishing on Autopilot

> Built for video creators, 3D animators, educators, and businesses to automate bulk scheduling across **YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, X (Twitter), Threads, Pinterest, and TikTok** with **zero monthly subscription fees**.

---

## ✨ Features That Stand Out

1. **Smart "Drip-Feed" Bulk Cadence Scheduler**:
   - Add 5, 10, or 50 videos at once today.
   - Choose your cadence: *"Publish 3 videos a week on Monday, Wednesday, and Friday at 6:30 PM IST"*.
   - In **1 single click**, ClipPilot calculates and distributes the exact future schedule dates for all videos across the coming weeks!

2. **Omnichannel Multi-Platform Publishing**:
   - 🔴 **YouTube**: Long-form videos and Shorts with custom thumbnails and playlist integration.
   - 🟣 **Instagram**: Reels and video posts via official Meta Graph API.
   - 🔵 **Facebook**: Page Reels and high-resolution videos.
   - 🔷 **LinkedIn**: Professional video posts.
   - 🐦 **X (Twitter)**: Video tweets.
   - 🧵 **Threads**: Video threads.
   - 📌 **Pinterest**: Video Pins.
   - 🎵 **TikTok**: Video sharing.

3. **Multilingual AI SEO Generator (English + Hindi + Hinglish)**:
   - Tailored specifically for Indian and global creators.
   - Generates high-CTR titles, timestamps, descriptions, and viral Instagram captions with high-reach hashtags in 1 click.

4. **Zero Cloud Middleman & 100% Privacy**:
   - Runs locally on your Windows PC.
   - Your multi-gigabyte video renders never pass through costly 3rd-party cloud servers.
   - Resumes uploads automatically if Wi-Fi or broadband drops.

5. **Auto-Pairing Drop Folder**:
   - Detects paired thumbnails (`video1.png`) and notes (`video1.txt`) automatically when you add videos.

---

## 🚀 How to Run

Double-click `run_clippilot.bat` or run:

```bash
cd OmniPost_Studio
python app.py
```

Then open `http://127.0.0.1:8765` in your browser or use the native desktop window.
